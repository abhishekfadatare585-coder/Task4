# Task4 – Severity & Case Classification

## Objective
To classify adverse drug reactions based on seriousness and expectedness.

| Case | Drug | Adverse Reaction | Seriousness | Expectedness | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | Amoxicillin | Skin rash, itching | Non-serious | Expected | Skin rash and itching are known adverse reactions associated with amoxicillin and do not indicate hospitalization or another seriousness criterion in the given case. |
| 02 | Ibuprofen | Gastrointestinal bleeding requiring hospitalization | Serious | Expected | The reaction required hospitalization, which meets a seriousness criterion. Gastrointestinal bleeding is a known adverse effect associated with NSAIDs such as ibuprofen. |
| 03 | Metformin | Lactic acidosis | Serious | Expected | Lactic acidosis is a serious, potentially life-threatening adverse reaction associated with metformin. It is a recognized risk described in metformin safety information. |

## Case 01 – Amoxicillin

**Adverse Reaction:** Skin rash and itching  
**Seriousness:** Non-serious  
**Expectedness:** Expected  

**Reason:** The reaction does not meet a seriousness criterion based on the information provided, and skin rash/itching are recognized adverse reactions of amoxicillin.

## Case 02 – Ibuprofen

**Adverse Reaction:** Gastrointestinal bleeding requiring hospitalization  
**Seriousness:** Serious  
**Expectedness:** Expected  

**Reason:** Hospitalization makes the case serious. Gastrointestinal bleeding is a recognized adverse reaction associated with ibuprofen and other NSAIDs.

## Case 03 – Metformin

**Adverse Reaction:** Lactic acidosis  
**Seriousness:** Serious  
**Expectedness:** Expected  

**Reason:** Lactic acidosis is a serious and potentially life-threatening adverse reaction associated with metformin and is a recognized safety risk.

## Summary

- Case 01: **Non-serious – Expected**
- Case 02: **Serious – Expected**
- Case 03: **Serious – Expected**
